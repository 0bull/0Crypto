**Lightning** is a fast way to pay in Bitcoin. 0Crypto takes Lightning payments through a wallet you already run with a hosted provider such as Alby Hub or Coinos. It connects with **Nostr Wallet Connect** (NWC), a standard way for an app to ask a wallet for invoices.

The money lands in your wallet and stays there. 0Crypto never sweeps, routes or refunds Lightning payments, and it never pays anything. It cannot be routed through privacy routing either.

## Connect your wallet

<Steps>

1. In your Lightning wallet create a new NWC connection. Give it **receive-only** permissions: the ability to create invoices (`make_invoice`), look them up (`lookup_invoice`) and read basic info (`get_info`). Nothing that can send. Menu names can differ by wallet.

2. Copy the connection string. It starts with `nostr+walletconnect://`. Treat it as a secret.

3. In the 0Crypto folder on your computer run `npx wrangler secret put NWC_URI` and paste the string. Or add it as a secret named `NWC_URI` in the Cloudflare dashboard under Variables and Secrets.

4. In your admin open **Settings**. In the **Coins** box tick **BTC on Lightning**, then press **Save wallets and coins** and confirm with your passkey. The box stays grey until the `NWC_URI` secret is set.

</Steps>

## Why receive-only matters

The connection string works like a key. If it leaks, someone with a receive-only connection can see and create invoices but cannot take your money. A connection that can spend could be drained. 0Crypto only ever uses the three receiving actions. It checks the connection before the first invoice and every 24 hours:

- If it cannot create and look up invoices, Lightning is left out of checkout and your Overview shows a `lightning.config_error` alert.
- If it also allows spending, Lightning still works, but a `lightning.spend_permission` alert asks you to swap it for a receive-only one. Do that.

## How a payment goes

The customer picks Lightning and sees a QR code with a countdown of 20 minutes. When it runs out, a **New invoice** button appears. 0Crypto asks your wallet whether it was paid, and credits the invoice only when the wallet confirms the exact amount.

Lightning invoices have a fixed amount, so they cannot be underpaid. If a customer pays twice (an old and a new invoice), the invoice shows as overpaid. There are no Lightning refunds in 0Crypto, so send the difference back from your wallet.

## Try it first

Create a $1 test invoice, pick Lightning and pay it from a different wallet. It should show as paid within about 30 seconds.

:::tip[You should now see]
Lightning appears as a choice at checkout, with no `lightning.config_error` or `lightning.spend_permission` alert on your Overview, and your $1 test invoice turns paid.
:::
