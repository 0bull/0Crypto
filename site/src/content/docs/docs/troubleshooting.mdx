## I can't find the setup code

The code is printed in your Worker's logs, not shown on the page.

1. On the setup page press **Print a new code**.
2. Open the Cloudflare dashboard, go to Workers & Pages, open **0crypto**, then Logs, and look for the new line that starts `0crypto setup code:` (a recovery code starts `0crypto recovery code:`).
3. If nothing appears, wait a moment and refresh the Logs view, and check that you opened the Worker called **0crypto** in the right Cloudflare account.

A code is 6 words and is valid for 60 minutes. You can print a new one at most once every 2 minutes. Up to 3 live codes exist at a time, and printing a fourth cancels the oldest. After 5 wrong tries from your address in 10 minutes the page says "Too many wrong codes. Wait 10 minutes and try again.", so wait and try again. Read more on [Find your setup code](/docs/setup-code/).

## My browser doesn't offer a passkey

- Open the gateway at its full `https://` address. Passkeys only work over a secure connection (the page says "Open the gateway over https://"), and only on a domain name, not on an IP address.
- Use an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox) on a device with a screen lock, Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key.
- Turn off a private window or a browser extension that blocks passkeys, then try again. Some password managers take over the passkey prompt. Choose "this device" or "security key" there if offered.
- Passkey steps need JavaScript. If a page says an action needs JavaScript, enable it for your gateway.
- If you changed the address of your gateway since you made the passkey, use **Lost your passkey or moved to a new domain?** on the sign-in page. See [Use your own domain](/docs/custom-domain/).

## A coin is greyed out

A coin is unavailable until what it needs is in place.

- **Bitcoin, Litecoin, Dogecoin:** paste a valid public key in the Coins step or in Settings. Dogecoin also needs a Blockbook address.
- **Ethereum chains, Tron, Solana:** these need [routed mode](/docs/advanced/routed-mode/) and the settings listed in [Ethereum, Tron and Solana](/docs/advanced/evm-tron-solana/). An Ethereum chain also needs its factory deployed. Open the Wallets page: it says what is missing for each chain.
- **Lightning:** needs a receive-only connection. See [Accept Lightning](/docs/advanced/lightning/).

## The first address doesn't match my wallet

Stop and do not accept payments yet. You may have copied the key from the wrong account or the wrong address type. Copy it again. See [Add your wallet](/docs/wallet/).

## Payments don't show up

- Check the blockchain explorer for the address. The customer may have paid the wrong coin or the wrong network. For wrong Ethereum chains, use **Recover deposit** on the invoice.
- If you made the gateway recently, check that you are on the Workers Paid plan.
- On Ethereum chains check that the provider connection address works. The Wallets page shows chain problems.

## Webhooks aren't arriving

- Your webhook URL must be `https://` and reachable from the internet.
- Your website must answer with a success status quickly. Failed deliveries are retried with longer waits, up to 20 times.
- Press **Send test event** on **API keys & webhooks** to see what your website answers.
- Events from before you saved a webhook URL are not sent afterwards.
- If you rotated the secret, update your website straight away. Messages signed with the new secret fail your check until you do.

## Sweeps are stuck

An alert on your Overview names the reason. The most common one is `sweep.insufficient_gas`: a hot wallet is out of fuel. Top it up. See [Ethereum, Tron and Solana](/docs/advanced/evm-tron-solana/).

## I still need help

Look in the repository's issues on GitHub, and include any alert text. Never post your seed phrase, API keys, webhook secret or setup code.
